ApexPlanet Task 4 – Data Storytelling & Statistical Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from 2024–2025 to identify business trends, regional performance, product performance, and the relationship between discounts and profit margins.

Dataset

- Total Records: 1,200
- Total Columns: 13
- Date Range: January 2024 – December 2025
- Missing Values: 0
- Duplicate Rows: 0

Tools & Technologies

- Matplotlib, Seaborn
- SciPy

Key Business Metrics

- Total Sales: ₹71.40 Million
- Total Profit: ₹10.72 Million
- Total Quantity Sold: 5,480
- Overall Profit Margin: 15.01%

Statistical Analysis

A Welch's independent two-sample t-test was performed to compare average profit margins across discount groups.

- Low Discount (0–5%): 17.03% average profit margin
- High Discount (10–20%): 13.97% average profit margin
- P-value: 1.66 × 10⁻¹⁸

The analysis found a statistically significant difference between the two groups. Correlation analysis also showed a negative association between discount and profit margin (r = -0.295).

Note: Statistical association does not establish causation.
